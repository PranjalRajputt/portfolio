Four projects, from mock interviews to hate speech detection.

Persona.ai

AI interview trainer

A full-stack mock interview platform with a UE5.7 MetaHuman avatar, real-time lip sync and multi-turn interview logic powered by Groq AI. Pixel Streaming brings the 3D interview into the browser with low latency. Frontend on Vercel, backend on Railway via Docker, data on Supabase.

React, Node.js, Groq AI, UE5, Python

BlindNav

Hackaccino hackathon project

Indoor navigation for blind students using WiFi fingerprinting with a KNN classifier, plus real-time obstacle detection with a COCO-SSD TFLite model. Spoken cues are throttled to one every 4 seconds so guidance stays clear.

TypeScript, TFLite, expo-speech

HateMM

Vision-language moderation

A 3-modality hate speech classifier (image, text and OCR text) trained on MMHS150K, 149,823 posts. A custom Cross-Modal Transformer with Modality-Gated Attention keeps fusion interpretable. A 3-phase pipeline fixed multimodal collapse and reached 75.96% accuracy and 0.714 AUROC.

PyTorch, ViT-B/16, RoBERTa

Meeting Transcription Platform

Fireflies.ai clone

Transcribes and summarizes meetings from Google Meet, Zoom and Microsoft Teams, with AI summaries, action items and topic chapters from LLaMA-3.1 on Groq. Includes a synced transcript and audio player, PDF and Markdown export, and a local fallback during API outages.

React, Node.js, Groq API, Python
